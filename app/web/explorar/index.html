Morelos, Mexico

Tepozteco Valley & Bougainvillea · Warm valleys & summer rains

Morelos is getting steadily warmer (+1.3 °C over 25 years). Even though yearly rain totals bounce up and down without a permanent drop, the extra heat is slowly drying out the top layer of soil.
