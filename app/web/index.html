OBSERVED VARIABLES & FIELD REGIONS · 2000–2024

Study regions

Six area-averaged observation boxes chosen by data rather than climate stereotypes — even a wet rainforest can dry significantly while a semi-arid belt wets. Each region is evaluated across three NASA Earth-science variables:

mm / year NASA GPM IMERG V07

Precipitation

Monthly satellite rain-rate estimates calibrated with rain gauges, summed over complete 12-month calendar years.

DOI: 10.5067/GPM/IMERG/3B-MONTH/07 ↗

Soil moisture

Water stored in the top 10 cm of soil, modelled by the GLDAS Noah Land Surface Model fed with satellite observations.

°C at 2 meters NASA MERRA-2 V5.12.4

STATISTICAL RIGOR · WHY THIS MATTERS

How we separate signal from climate noise

A short 25-year window can fool standard regressions when El Niño or soil memory links one year to the next. Atlalli applies a strict protocol across all 18 series.

01 · MAGNITUDE

Sen’s Non-Parametric Slope

Reports the median rate of change per decade with a 95% confidence interval, remaining resistant to extreme drought or flood outlier years.

02 · AUTOCORRELATION RULE

Mann-Kendall & Hamed-Rao

Tests monotonic trends and switches to Hamed-Rao variance correction whenever positive lag-1 serial correlation exceeds 0.20.

03 · MULTIPLE TESTING

Benjamini-Hochberg FDR (q ≤ 0.05)

All 18 planned comparisons (3 variables × 6 regions) are corrected together so random fluctuations are not misreported as discoveries.
